# Mistele et al. (2024) weak-lensing RAR table

`mistele2024_weak_lensing_rar_table1.csv` is a machine-readable transcription
of Table 1 in:

T. Mistele, S. McGaugh, F. Lelli, J. Schombert and P. Li, “Radial
Acceleration Relation of Galaxies with Joint Kinematic and Weak-Lensing
Data”, *JCAP* 04 (2024) 020, DOI
[`10.1088/1475-7516/2024/04/020`](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2024/04/020).

The final publisher version is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). The source record and
license statement are available from Case Western Reserve University:
<https://commons.case.edu/facultyworks/800/>.

The four columns reproduce the published values of `log10(g_bar)`,
`log10(g_obs)`, the statistical uncertainty on `log10(g_obs)`, and the
systematic uncertainty attributed to interpolation/extrapolation in the
deprojection. Accelerations are in metres per second squared. The article
also reports a fixed additional 0.1 dex systematic on `log10(g_obs)` from
stellar-mass uncertainty; that value is not inserted into the source table
and is instead applied explicitly by the DarkPipe v0.11 operator.

This CSV is a curated transcription, not a byte-identical upstream download
and not raw KiDS survey data. Cite the article when reusing it. DarkPipe's
software remains licensed `GPL-3.0-or-later`; this data transcription retains
the source article's CC BY 4.0 attribution requirement.
