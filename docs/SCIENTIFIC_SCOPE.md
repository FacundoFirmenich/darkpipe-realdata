# Scientific scope and claim ledger

## Governing objective

Build a real-data-first, projection-aware pipeline for environmental foregrounds and sensor-level recovery steps required by a future controlled search for transient or oscillatory dark-sector signatures.

## Established through 0.4

1. Bounded NOAA SWPC and USGS acquisition with byte provenance.
2. Alignment, declared nuisance projection and residual diagnostics.
3. Authentic selected AION deposit with 27/27 hashes and separate licenses.
4. Preregistered 0.1–100 mHz injected-frequency recovery: 7/7 within one Fourier cell.
5. Frozen HLN−LLN interval includes zero; consistency, not causal equivalence.
6. Checked receipt reproduces without the 1.196 GB raw archive.

## Established in 0.5

1. New AION and NOAA–USGS receipts carry typed observation envelopes and claim ledgers.
2. Observational evidence promotes only observations/associations.
3. Causal, detection, generalization and intervention claims remain `NOT_ESTIMABLE` without their gates.
4. Decouplings cannot silently acquire cause, duration, meaning, intent or provisionality.
5. Conducence is contextual/vectorial; no scalar collapse.
6. v0.4 endpoint values remain unchanged.
7. Genealogy is public only as hashes/counts; one 20,000-character truncation is explicit and raw text remains private.

## Not established

- Dark plasma, ultralight-field, topological-defect or gravitational-wave detection.
- Blind false-positive rate or trials-corrected global significance.
- Full raw-HDF5 marginal-likelihood reconstruction.
- Transfer to AION-10, AION-km or another facility.
- Causation from NOAA-USGS lag/coherence.
- Generalization across instruments, epochs or sites.
- Universality of the authority architecture.

## Required next gate

Run a blind or held-out null/injection campaign with calibrated trials, raw phase-extraction inputs, frozen nuisance hierarchy, quality cuts, signal family and band, coverage checks and independent replication.
