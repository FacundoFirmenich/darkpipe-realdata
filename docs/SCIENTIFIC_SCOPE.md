# Scientific scope and claim ledger

## Governing objective

Build a real-data-first, projection-aware pipeline for identifying environmental foregrounds and validating sensor-level recovery steps required by a future, controlled search for transient or oscillatory dark-sector signatures in precision gradiometric data.

## Established in 0.3.0

1. Current official NOAA SWPC and USGS environmental observations can be acquired within hard byte ceilings with exact transport provenance.
2. Measured solar-wind nuisance channels can be aligned with a terrestrial geomagnetic target and projected before residual spectral/non-Gaussian diagnostics.
3. The lightweight environmental workflow runs on ordinary CPU hardware.

## Newly established in 0.4.0

1. A bounded authentic AION differential atom-interferometer deposit can be ingested with 27/27 byte hashes, explicit schemas and separate data/software license jurisdictions.
2. Under preregistration `DP-AION-0.4-20260825`, seven intentionally injected coherent phase-modulation frequencies from 0.1 to 100 mHz are recovered within one Fourier-resolution cell (7/7).
3. The upstream HLN and LLN differential-phase uncertainty derivatives give an HLN−LLN interval that includes zero under the frozen rule; this is consistent with no resolved excess from added laser phase noise within this representation.
4. The complete checked receipt reproduces locally and in the test suite without downloading the 1.196 GB raw archive.

## Not established

- Existence or detection of dark plasma, ultralight fields, topological defects or gravitational waves.
- A blind discovery-search false-positive rate or trials-corrected global significance.
- Independent reconstruction of the complete upstream marginal-likelihood pipeline from raw HDF5.
- Transfer of tabletop 1 mm-baseline behavior to AION-10, AION-km or another facility.
- Long-baseline transfer functions, clock/systematics closure, astrophysical sensitivity or exclusion limits.
- Generalization across instruments, epochs or sites.

The selected AION files contain intentional positive controls and published noise-rejection derivatives. They do not constitute an unblinded candidate event dataset.

## Required next scientific gate

Run a genuinely blind or held-out null/injection campaign with a complete trials-calibrated null ensemble and raw phase-extraction inputs. Predefine nuisance hierarchy, quality cuts, signal family, band, multiple-testing correction and coverage checks. Then replicate the fixed protocol on an independent instrument or observing interval before any discovery or exclusion claim.
